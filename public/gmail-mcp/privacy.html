gmail-mcp privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

gmail-mcp is a personal tool used only by Matthew Amiadamen with his own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With permission given on Google's consent screen, it can read messages, create drafts, and manage labels and filters in the connected accounts. It cannot send email.

How data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on Matthew's own computer.
  • Email content is fetched from Google when requested. Downloaded attachments are saved on the same computer.
  • When Matthew asks his AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, about his email, the relevant content is sent to Claude to answer that request.
  • Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
